Crowdscape

A live digital installation that turns an audience into a moving collective portrait and can culminate in a prize draw revealed in front of the crowd.

Core idea

Everyone becomes part of the scene.

Each visitor is captured by a camera and appears as a textured 3D object inside a shared virtual space. As more people join, the screen grows into a living archive of the crowd.

01

A visitor steps in front of the camera and an operator takes their portrait.

02

Spawn

03

Accumulate

The scene keeps growing, turning everyone who joined into part of one evolving visual.

Live activation

Live prize draw.

Every participant receives a unique number when they enter the scene. During the live reveal, Crowdscape selects one portrait, brings it into focus and announces the winner on screen in front of the audience.

JoinA portrait enters the scene with a unique number.
SelectThe system randomly chooses one participant.
RevealThe winning portrait and number move into focus.
