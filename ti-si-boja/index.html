How it works

Move

A camera reads the participant's silhouette, trajectory and changing pace.

Become

The body is translated into living digital paint that spreads, mixes and builds in layers.

No interface

No brush, controller or prescribed gesture. The participant enters the work simply by moving.

TI SI BOJA in action

See the body become paint.

The vertical demonstration follows the encounter from physical movement to a temporary digital painting unfolding in real time.

TI SI BOJA means “You are color.”

A digital material

Paint with physical presence.

The system was developed specifically to make virtual paint feel dense, dimensional and tactile. It mixes, flows and accumulates in layers rather than behaving like ordinary screen graphics or a particle effect.

TI SI BOJA is first and foremost an art project by Artemy (Zen) Naidenov. Its palette, scale and presentation can also be adapted to the architecture and visual context of an event or exhibition while preserving the original artistic premise.

Temporary paintings

Every encounter leaves a different trace.

Slow movements create broad, fluid marks. Fast gestures produce sharper, more energetic forms. No composition repeats in exactly the same way.

Open interaction

No correct way to move.

The installation does not ask visitors to reproduce an image or learn a control scheme. Each person discovers the material through their own pace, posture and gesture.

A living artwork

Present for one encounter.

Each composition exists for a limited time. The paint gradually disappears, releasing the canvas for the next participant and making every meeting with the work unrepeatable.

  • Created through presence
  • Different for every participant
  • Built from layered movement
  • Designed to fade and begin again
